DeHub Godmode Handbook — moderator and admin manual

Panel godmode.dehub.io For moderators & admins Reading time ~12 minutes

This is the whole job, in plain words. You do not need to know how any of it is built. Read it once end to end, then keep it open on your second screen for the first week.

// the golden rules

  1. Only malicious actors get banned.

    People gaming the system, scamming users, and the like. A person being disliked, wrong, rude or unpopular is not a malicious actor. If you cannot name the harm they are doing to somebody else, it is not a ban.

  2. Adult or extreme content gets marked mature, not deleted.

    It is allowed on DeHub. Marking it mature keeps it off the public feed while leaving it on the creator's profile for the people who came for it. Reach for Mark mature first, every time.

  3. The only thing deleted without hesitation is child sexual abuse material.

    Everything else is left to community moderation. If you see this, delete it and tell a super admin immediately — do not investigate it yourself, do not discuss it in a group chat, and do not download anything.

When a rule below seems to point one way and a golden rule points the other, the golden rule wins. If you are about to do something these three do not cover, that is the moment to ask rather than decide.

// 01  —  what this thing is

The panel

Godmode is DeHub's community control room. It is a separate website from dehub.io — you sign in with a moderator account, not with your wallet, and it is not reachable from the app.

There is no staff here. DeHub is community owned and community run, and this room is the community looking after itself. You are not an employee policing customers; you are a member of the same place everyone else is in, holding the door. That is the whole reason the rules below lean so hard towards doing less.

From it you can:

  • look at any user, post, comment, stage or chat message on the platform;
  • hide, rate or remove content that breaks the rules;
  • stop someone posting, commenting, or using the platform at all;
  • answer support tickets and clear the reports queue;
  • announce things to everybody at once.

// the one thing to remember

Every button you press is written to an audit log with your name on it — what you did, to what, when, and the note you typed. That log is a good thing: it protects you when a decision is questioned later. Write your reasons as if someone will read them, because someone will.

// 02  —  first time in

Getting in

You cannot sign yourself up. Somebody already moderating invites you, and the invite arrives as an email with a link that expires after seven days.

  1. Open the invite link. It takes you to a page called Complete Your Account.
  2. Set your name and password. Minimum eight characters, with at least one capital, one lowercase and one number.
  3. Set up two-factor. Scan the square code with an authenticator app — Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password, whichever you already use — and type the six digits it shows you.
  4. Save your recovery codes. You get a one-time list of codes. Screenshot them, print them, put them in your password manager. This is the only time you will ever see them.
  5. Sign in. Go to godmode.dehub.io, enter email and password, then the six digits from your app.

// if you lose your phone

On the code screen, type one of your recovery codes instead of the six digits. Each code works once and is then dead. When you are back in, go to Profile → Two-factor authentication and issue a new set.

Forgotten password

Click Forgot password on the sign-in page and enter your email. Nothing happens automatically — a senior admin has to approve the request on the Admins page and send you back a reset link. Message them so they know it is waiting.

Housekeeping

  • Sign out when you finish a shift, especially on a shared machine.
  • Your moderator password is not your dehub.io password. Do not reuse one for the other.
  • Never send anyone a screenshot of a user's email address or wallet.

// 03  —  what you can and cannot touch

Your seat

There are four seats. Yours decides which pages appear in the left sidebar and which buttons appear on them. If a button you have read about here is not on your screen, it is not a bug — your seat does not have it.

SeatWhat it is forCan doCannot do
Viewer An outside observer. Safe to hand to a partner or investor. Dashboard, uploads, stages, categories. Sees no users, no reports, no support tickets, no emails. The server refuses those, not just the screen.
Moderator Day-to-day content work. This is most people. Users, uploads, stages, comments, reports, support, global chat. Hide, flag, pin, delete comments, restrict a user's posting or commenting. No banning, no settings, no admin management, no user email addresses.
Admin Keeps the moderator group running. Everything a moderator can, plus deleting posts, rating posts mature, ads review, categories, broadcast, inviting other moderators, and the audit log. Still no banning, no platform settings, no server logs, no user email addresses.
Super admin The keys to the building. All of the above, plus banning, platform settings and maintenance mode, changing anyone's role, and seeing user email addresses. —

// banning is deliberately rare

Only a super admin can ban, and that is deliberate. Golden rule 1 sets the bar: a ban is for malicious actors — gaming the system, scamming users — not for people who are wrong or unpleasant. A ban stands until a person lifts it, so it is always a considered decision. Gather the evidence and hand it up.

// 05  —  a normal shift

The daily round

Four queues, in this order. Twenty minutes twice a day clears most days.

1  Reports

This is what users have flagged. The page splits into content reports (a post) and user reports (a person). Open the oldest pending one, look at the actual post or profile, then pick an action.

On a postWhat it does
RemoveTakes the post down.
Warn creatorSends them your reason as a notification. Nothing else changes.
DismissThe report was wrong or the post is fine. Closes it.
On a personWhat it does
WarnA notification with your reason. Start here for a first offence.
Restrict postingThey can still read and comment, but cannot post.
Restrict commentsThey can still post, but cannot comment.
Ban super adminLocks them out of posting, commenting, voting and following.
DismissNothing to answer. Closes it.

Every one of these asks for two boxes. Reason is sent to the user — write it so a stranger would understand it. Admin note is internal and never leaves the panel — put the evidence there.

2  Comments

Filter by Flagged first — the platform automatically flags comments containing suspicious links, and those land here. Per comment you can Hide, Flag, Pin, Edit or Delete. Tick several and act on them together when one person has spammed a thread.

  • Hide is the gentle one — the comment stops showing, and you can put it back.
  • Delete is for scams, targeted abuse and spam rings. Prefer Hide whenever you are unsure — a disagreement is not abuse.
  • Pin sticks a comment to the top of a post. Useful for a creator's own correction.

3  Support

Real people waiting for an answer. Work Urgent and High first, then oldest first. Set the status as you go: Open → In progress → Resolved → Closed.

The reply box is shown to them in the app and read back word for word by the DeHub assistant, so write full sentences, not shorthand. The internal note box is never shown to anyone outside this group.

4  Global Chat

DeHub has one platform-wide chat room. Four tabs: Participants (who is in it — make someone a room moderator or ban them from here), Messages (delete or pin any message), Moderators, and Banned. A chat ban only affects chat; it does not touch their account.

// 06  —  choosing the right tool

Dealing with a post

Three different buttons look similar and do very different things. This table is the most useful thing in the handbook.

ButtonWhat happensUse it whenReversible
Mark mature The post drops out of public discovery — home feed, shorts, search, suggestions. It stays on the author's own profile and anyone with the link can still open it. Adults who opted in still see it. Golden rule 2 — any adult or extreme content. It is allowed on DeHub; it just should not greet a stranger on the home page. This is your default, not your last resort. Yes — Mark safe
Hide The post disappears from every surface, including the author's own profile. You need it gone right now while you check something. Yes — Show
Delete super admin A takedown. The post comes off the platform, and a super admin can restore it if the decision is overturned. Rarely. Golden rule 3 — child sexual abuse material is deleted on sight. For anything else, reach for Mark mature or Hide and ask. By a super admin

// a rating you set is final

A creator can mark their own post mature, and change their mind. The moment a moderator sets the rating, it is frozen — their edit screen refuses to change it. So be sure before you use it, and put your reasoning in the note.

Reading the Uploads page

  • Published is the default filter and means "live right now". It covers both on-chain and off-chain posts.
  • Off-chain on a post is normal — the creator chose not to mint it. It is not an error and not "pending".
  • AI Auto-Categorize re-files a post under better tags. It never changes the content.

// 07  —  escalating on a person

Dealing with a person

Open them from Users, or from any report. Work down this ladder, and only go as far as you need to.

StepActionThey can still…
01WarnEverything. They just get a notification.
02Remove from home feedPost, comment, and be found by their followers and on their profile — their posts just stop appearing in public discovery.
03Disable commentsPost, browse, watch.
04Disable postingBrowse, watch, comment.

// give it a moment

A restriction takes a short moment to reach every part of the platform. Give it a minute and refresh before assuming it did not apply.

Sockpuppets — one person, many accounts

On a user's page there is a Linked accounts card listing other accounts seen on the same devices or the same internet connection. Read it carefully:

  • Shared a device is a strong signal. The same phone or the same browser profile. Treat it as the same person unless there is a good reason not to.
  • Shared an IP only is a weak signal. Mobile networks, offices, universities and shared houses all put strangers on one address. Never act on this alone.

The device signal is not available for every account, so an empty card is not proof of anything either way. When you do find a ring, gather the whole list first and hand it to a super admin in one go — working through them one at a time over an hour just teaches them to spread out.

Blocked devices

Banning can also block the device behind the account, which stops it signing up again. The Blocked Devices page is that list — check it when someone insists they cannot create an account, and unblock if they were caught by mistake.

// 08  —  the ones that touch everybody

Heavy levers

Four controls affect people who were not part of the problem. Read the whole entry before you touch one.

Broadcast admin +

Sends one notification to every user on DeHub. Title up to 100 characters, message up to 500. There is a confirm step, and afterwards a summary of how many were reached. Treat it as a platform-wide announcement, not a marketing channel — reserve it for things everyone genuinely needs to know.

  • Choose Both for anything genuinely important; mobile app users get a phone push, web users get the in-app bell.
  • Proofread twice. There is no unsend, and no edit.
  • If you are linking to a blog post, paste the full link into the article field rather than into the message.

Maintenance mode super admin

This takes the entire platform offline — web and mobile, every user, until someone turns it off. It asks you to type the word MAINTENANCE precisely so it cannot be clicked by accident. Only ever use it when engineering asks you to.

The other Settings switches super admin

Video uploads, livestreaming and comments each have a platform-wide on/off. Turning one off stops it for everybody, immediately. Change one only when you have been asked to — never to find out what it does.

Ads review admin +

Campaigns and their individual creatives wait here for approval. Approve puts them in front of users; Reject sends your review note back to the advertiser, so write something they can act on rather than "no".

// 09  —  how we work

House rules

  • Always fill in the reason. The user sees it. A restriction with no explanation generates a support ticket, an argument, and often a reversal.
  • Put the evidence in the internal note. Post IDs, comment text, the report that started it. Future-you will not remember.
  • Prefer the reversible action. Hide before delete, restrict before ban. You can always escalate tomorrow; you cannot un-send a ban that went out to the wrong person.
  • Let the community do the moderating. Outside the golden rules, an unpopular post is for downvotes and replies to settle, not for you. Doing less is usually the right call.
  • Never moderate yourself into a dispute you are part of. If the person has argued with you personally, hand it to another moderator.
  • Do not share what you see here. Emails, wallets, personal details, ticket contents — none of it leaves the panel. Not in a screenshot, not in a group chat.
  • When in doubt, escalate. Nobody has ever been told off for asking. Plenty of platforms have been embarrassed by a moderator who guessed.

// 10  —  before you raise it

If something looks off

What you seeWhat it meansWhat to do
A button you expected is missing Your seat does not have it. Nothing is broken. Check section 03. Ask an admin if you think it is wrong.
A page says you do not have access Same thing, from the server's side. Nothing. It is working as designed.
A page shows an error you do not recognise Something to pass on rather than work around. Screenshot it with the page name and the time, and send it to the group. Do not keep retrying.
You get signed out These sessions are deliberately short. Sign in again.
A restriction does not seem to have applied It takes a short moment to reach the whole platform. Wait a minute and refresh before doing it a second time.
A post shows a "mature" tag you did not set The creator marked it themselves when posting — often a mis-tap. If the content is plainly not adult, Mark safe and leave a note.
A figure here does not match what you see on dehub.io The two are measured over different windows. Ask before acting on it, and never quote a figure from here outside this group.

// still stuck

Take a screenshot, note the exact time, and say what you were trying to do. Those three things turn a twenty-message thread into a two-message one.
